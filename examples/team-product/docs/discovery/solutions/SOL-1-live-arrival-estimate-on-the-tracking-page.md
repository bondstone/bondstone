## Idea

Show a two-hour arrival window on the tracking page and narrow it as the parcel moves
through the network.

## Assumptions

- Customers trust a window more than a single time.
- Scan events arrive quickly enough for the window to stay accurate.
- An estimate that is sometimes wrong still reduces contacts overall.
