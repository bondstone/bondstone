assumption: An arrival window that is sometimes wrong still reduces location contacts
success_criteria: Location contacts drop by at least 20% compared with the control group
---
## Method

Four weeks in Gdańsk. Half of the parcels showed a two-hour window computed from
historical route times; the other half showed the usual last-scan page.

## Result

Location contacts fell by 31% in the test group. The window was wrong for 12% of parcels,
and those parcels generated no more contacts than the control group. The assumption holds.
